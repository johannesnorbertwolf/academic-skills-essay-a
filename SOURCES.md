`sources/` folder of the working copy, but that folder is **not committed**, for
copyright reasons — these are course and publisher materials, not ours to
redistribute. This file is the public record of what was used.

## How to cite them

The two papers are already entered in `refs.bib`, so citing them in `essay.md`
is just `[@miyatsu2018]` or `[@putnam2016]`, and the reference list is built
automatically in APA style.
