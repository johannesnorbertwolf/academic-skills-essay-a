Yes — it's the APA heading-level system, not a mistake. APA defines five heading levels, each a different visual style, and the style tells you how deeply a heading is nested:

- Level 1 is centred and bold (a top-level section). - Level 2 is flush left and bold (a subsection of the level above it). - Levels 3–5 go further down (italic, then indented with a full stop).
